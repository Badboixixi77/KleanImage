# Owner Handover Guide — Klean Image Website

This guide explains how to manage your website without needing a developer.

---

## Part 1: Editing Content in Sanity

Sanity is the tool you use to update your website content — services, prices, gallery photos, and more.

### How to log in

1. Open your browser and go to your Sanity Studio URL (e.g. `https://kleanimage.sanity.studio`)
2. Sign in with the email address you used when setting up the site

### Editing a service (price, description, turnaround)

1. Click **"Service"** in the left menu
2. Click the service you want to edit (e.g. "Business Cards")
3. Change the fields you want to update:
   - **Starting Price** — enter the price in Naira (numbers only, no ₦ symbol needed)
   - **Turnaround** — e.g. "2–3 business days"
   - **Description** — write what the service includes
   - **Min. Order Quantity** — the minimum number of items
4. Click **Publish** (green button at the top right) to save changes
5. Your website will automatically update within 60 seconds

### Adding a gallery photo

1. Click **"Gallery Item"** in the left menu
2. Click **"+ New Gallery Item"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — a short name for the photo (e.g. "Wedding invitation set 2026")
   - **Image** — click to upload your photo
   - **Alt Text** — describe the image in a sentence (e.g. "Printed wedding invitations with gold foil finish")
   - **Category** — select the type of work (e.g. Print, Branded Merchandise)
   - **Featured on Home page** — tick this to show it on the home page
4. Click **Publish**

### Adding a testimonial (customer review)

1. Click **"Testimonial"** in the left menu
2. Click **"+ New Testimonial"**
3. Fill in the customer's name, their role or company, the review text, and a star rating
4. Click **Publish**

### Updating business hours or contact details

1. Click **"Site Settings"** in the left menu
2. Update the relevant field (e.g. phone, email, hours)
3. Click **Publish**

## Part 2: Managing Quotes in the Admin Dashboard

The admin dashboard is where you see all quote requests from customers.

### How to log in

1. Go to `https://yourdomain.com/admin/login`
2. Sign in with your admin email and password

### Viewing a quote request

1. On the **Quotes** page, you see all requests listed newest first
2. Click any row to see the full details:
   - Customer name, phone, email
   - Service requested, quantity, size, finish
   - Deadline and delivery method
   - Any artwork file they uploaded (click **Download** to view it)

### Updating a quote status

Each quote moves through these stages:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **New** | Just submitted — needs your attention |
| **Quoted** | You've sent them a price |
| **Confirmed** | Customer has confirmed the order |
| **In Production** | You're printing it |
| **Ready** | Finished and ready for collection/delivery |
| **Delivered** | Job complete |
| **Cancelled** | Order cancelled |

To update the status:
1. Open the quote
2. Select the new status from the dropdown
3. Optionally add a note (e.g. "Called customer, confirmed 500 cards")
4. Click **Save**

### Replying to a customer on WhatsApp

1. Open the quote
2. Click the **"Reply on WhatsApp"** button
3. WhatsApp will open with the customer's number and a pre-filled message — just add your quote price and send

### Exporting quotes to Excel

1. On the Quotes page, apply any filter you want (e.g. Status = Delivered)
2. Click **"Export CSV"**
3. Open the downloaded file in Excel or Google Sheets

## Part 3: What to Do If Something Looks Wrong

- **Website not updating after editing in Sanity?** Wait 60 seconds and refresh. If it still hasn't updated, contact your developer.
- **Can't log in to the admin?** Check your email for the password reset link or contact your developer.
- **A customer says they submitted a quote but you didn't receive it?** Check the Quotes dashboard — it should be there. Also check your email spam folder.
